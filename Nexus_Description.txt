[center][size=6][color=#00ccff][b]AbsoluteZero 1.4.0[/b][/color][/size]
[size=4][b][color=#ffaa00]Mouse alignment and locked-reticule steering for Starfield.[/color][/b][/size][/center]

AbsoluteZero gently returns ship mouse aim toward center after input stops. Its Locked Reticule mode keeps the crosshair centered while translating mouse motion into direct ship steering. It runs standalone and integrates with the shared Absolute Control menu.

[line]

[center][size=5][color=#ffcc00][b]Features[/b][/color][/size][/center]

[list]
[*][b]Idle mouse centering[/b] with configurable radius, delay, and exponential decay.[/*]
[*][b]Locked Reticule mode[/b] for centered-crosshair direct mouse steering with in-menu toggle and hotkey.[/*]
[*][b]Interactive centering lab[/b] that responds continuously to mouse movement and demonstrates the configured activation radius, idle delay, and return rate.[/*]
[*][b]Dedicated Bindings tab[/b] with support for keyboard shortcuts and physical DirectInput joystick buttons / POV hats.[/*]
[*][b]Center on press mode[/b] with its own binding as an alternative to automatic idle centering.[/*]
[*][b]Hold-to-suppress binding[/b] for temporary unrestricted mouse aim in automatic mode.[/*]
[*][b]Native MOD OPTIONS multi-tab page[/b] through Absolute Control, including binding capture and live settings.[/*]
[*][b]Headless fallback[/b] through the INI and live-tuning hotkeys.[/*]
[*][b]Safe HOTAS pairing[/b] through explicit axis ownership and backward-compatible shared writer integration.[/*]
[/list]

[line]

[center][size=5][color=#ffaa00][b]AbsoluteHOTAS compatibility[/b][/color][/size][/center]

AbsoluteZero coordinates with AbsoluteHOTAS safely without competing writer hooks. When both are installed, AbsoluteZero owns mouse pitch/yaw steering while HOTAS controls roll, strafe, throttle, buttons, and profiles through its shared writer seam.

A joystick cannot control pitch and yaw while AbsoluteZero is actively driving them. In paired mode, native idle centering remains fully supported.

[line]

[center][size=5][color=#00ccff][b]Requirements and installation[/b][/color][/size][/center]

[list]
[*]Starfield 1.16.242 or 1.16.244[/*]
[*][url=https://sfse.silverlock.org/]SFSE[/url] 0.2.20 or later[/*]
[*]No Address Library required[/*]
[*]Absolute Control is optional but recommended for the native in-game editor[/*]
[/list]

Install normally with MO2 or Vortex, or place [font=Courier New]AbsoluteZero.dll[/font] and [font=Courier New]AbsoluteZero.ini[/font] in [font=Courier New]Data\SFSE\Plugins\[/font].

With Absolute Control installed, open the Pause Menu, select [color=#ffaa00][b]MOD OPTIONS[/b][/color], and choose [b]AbsoluteZero[/b].

[line]

[center][size=5][color=#00ccff][b]Live tuning & shortcuts[/b][/color][/size][/center]

[list]
[*][font=Courier New]Alt+Numpad+ / Alt+Numpad-[/font] — radius ±2.0[/*]
[*][font=Courier New]Ctrl+Numpad+ / Ctrl+Numpad-[/font] — idle delay ±10 ms[/*]
[*][font=Courier New]Shift+Numpad+ / Shift+Numpad-[/font] — decay rate ±0.5[/*]
[*][font=Courier New]Ctrl+Alt+0[/font] — toggle idle centering[/*]
[*][font=Courier New]Alt+Numpad0[/font] — toggle Locked Reticule mode (default; remappable in MOD OPTIONS)[/*]
[*][font=Courier New]Ctrl+Alt+S[/font] — save current settings[/*]
[/list]

Logging is off by default and occurs only when [font=Courier New]bLogEnabled=true[/font].
